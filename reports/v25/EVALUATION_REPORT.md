## 3. Draws

Neither v2.4 nor v2.5 can separate draws. Under argmax, draw recall is ~0% because P(draw) never becomes the largest class probability; v2.5's maximum P(draw) on the test set is 0.296. Mean P(draw) is 0.235 on matches that were draws vs 0.231 on matches that were not (v2.4: 0.244 vs 0.237). The observed draw rate was 25.9%, versus a mean predicted 23.2%. No probability was inflated to force draw predictions: doing so would damage log loss and calibration without adding real information. Draw recall is therefore reported, but it is not a target.
